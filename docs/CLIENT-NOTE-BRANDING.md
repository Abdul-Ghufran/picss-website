**Subject:** PICSS website — five branding items for your confirmation

Dear colleagues,

The institutional website is built and ready for you to review. Before it goes live we need
your decision on five branding points. Four are quick confirmations; the first two are the
ones that genuinely need your view.

**1. The logo is missing a letter.**
The descriptor in the supplied artwork reads "Pakistan **Institue** for Conflict and Security
Studies" — *Institute* without its second *t*. We have left your artwork exactly as supplied
rather than altering it. The website's own text uses the correct spelling throughout. Please
confirm whether you would like the logo corrected, and if so, send the amended file.

**2. We have adjusted one letter of the logo for use on dark backgrounds.**
Your brand indigo is very dark, and against the site's dark navy header the "C" of PiCSS
effectively disappeared — the wordmark read as four letters rather than five. For dark
surfaces only, we have lightened that single letter to a brighter indigo so the full name
reads clearly. Your logo is used unchanged on light backgrounds. Producing a reversed variant
is standard practice, but as it touches your mark we would like your explicit approval.

**3. Could you send a vector version of the logo?**
We received a JPEG at 554 × 554 pixels. It works, but it cannot scale cleanly and carries
some compression artefacts at larger sizes. An SVG, AI or EPS original would noticeably
improve quality across the site, in print and for any future materials.

**4. Please confirm the colour palette.**
With no brand guidelines supplied, we sampled the colours directly from your logo: red
`#DC2A1C`, indigo `#3D2B9B` and the sky blue of the world map `#5CBDE0`. If official brand
colours exist, we will use those instead.

**5. The typefaces are our recommendation, not your standard.**
We have paired an editorial serif (Fraunces) for headlines with a clear sans-serif (Inter)
for body text, and a monospaced face for dates and data labels. If PICSS has a house
typeface, we will substitute it.

One further item: no share image has been designed yet for when the site is posted on social
media or messaging platforms. We are happy to produce one once the logo questions above are
settled.

We will hold these as they stand until we hear from you.

With best regards,
